FAQs
What’s the difference between linen, percale, and sateen?
Linen, percale, and sateen refer to different fabric feels and weaves. Linen is naturally textured, breathable, and cool with a relaxed, timeless look. Percale has a crisp, cool, matte finish with excellent airflow, perfect if you sleep warm. Sateen is smoother and softer with a subtle sheen and cozy drape, ideal if you like a richer, plush feel.

Does this bedding fit my mattress?
Our fitted sheets have a 15″ deep pocket, so they work with most standard or pillow-top mattresses. Check the size chart if you need exact dimensions before you choose.

Will this bedding feel different than what I’m used to?
Our fabrics use naturally breathable weaves and high-quality organic fibers, so they may feel softer, cooler, or more textured than conventional sheets. Many customers describe them as “crisp but cozy” (percale), “smooth and silky” (sateen), or “soft and relaxed” (linen). If you want a specific feel, check the fabric description above or contact us for help choosing.

How long will this bedding last?
Or & Zon bedding is crafted from long-lasting organic cotton and linen and designed to soften with time, not wear out quickly. With normal home care (gentle wash, low tumble dry or line dry), these textiles retain comfort and strength through many wash cycles, making them a product you can enjoy for years, not just months.

How should I care for my bedding?
Machine wash on gentle with cold or lukewarm water and mild detergent. Tumble dry on low or line dry. This keeps organic fibers soft and breathable wash after wash.
